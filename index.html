How it works

Six steps, each with an example. In most of them $EXAMPLE is a pond and $SPROUT is a fish living in it.

1 · The pond

Any coin can be a pond.

A pond is a coin that already trades on Robinhood Chain, with at least 1 ETH of liquidity. Anyone can register it, once. The pond takes the colour of the coin's logo.

Top on Pons right now
Hover a coin to see it as a pond.
2 · The fish

Drop a fish in any pond.

A fish is a new coin that lives in a pond. It is bought and sold with the pond's coin, not with ETH. Every fish is a koi, swimming in its pond.

ExampleYou launch $SPROUT in the $EXAMPLE pond: a picture, a name, the pond. People buy $SPROUT with $EXAMPLE. No $EXAMPLE? The site swaps ETH to $EXAMPLE and $EXAMPLE to $SPROUT in one click. Launching costs 2.5% of your first buy, nothing else.
3 · The fee

Every fish trade burns the pond.

Every buy or sell of a fish pays 1%, in the pond's coin. Half of it is burned forever, so the busier the fish, the smaller the pond's supply.

ExampleSomeone buys 1,000 EXAMPLE of $SPROUT. The fee is 10 EXAMPLE.
4 · Growing up

At 2 ETH of volume, a fish leaves the curve.

When a fish reaches 2 ETH of trading volume, it graduates. It moves to a Uniswap pool with 100% of its liquidity locked forever, and its trades keep feeding the pond. It can then become a pond itself, up to 3 deep.

Example$SPROUT has traded 2 ETH in total. Its liquidity is locked for good, and $SPROUT can now be a pond with fish of its own.
5 · Frenzy

Burn $PONDS, start a frenzy.

Anyone can burn $PONDS on any pond to start a frenzy. While it lasts, 70% of that pond's fees burn it instead of 50%, and the pond sits right next to the middle of the map.

Example$EXAMPLE pays the treasury about 0.004 ETH every 10 minutes. 10 minutes of frenzy costs 1.5 times that, so 0.006 ETH of $PONDS, burned. One hour costs 0.036 ETH. You can stack up to 24 hours.
6 · $PONDS

$PONDS burns itself too.

Every frenzy burns $PONDS. $PONDS is a pond as well, so every trade of a fish living in it burns $PONDS. And a steady buy and burn runs on its own, all the time. Nobody has to remember, nobody has to be trusted.
